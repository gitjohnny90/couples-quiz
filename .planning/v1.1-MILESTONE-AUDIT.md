## Tech Debt

### Phase 8: Quality

- **`useCallback` wrapping absent in 2 of 3 pages** — `PredictPartnerPage.fetchResponses` and `StudyTogetherPage.fetchData` are plain async functions passed to `useRealtimeSync`. This causes the hook's internal effects to re-run on every parent render, resetting the polling interval. `LoveNoteHuntPage` correctly wraps its callback. Fix: wrap both functions in `useCallback([sessionId])`. Not user-visible but departs from the established Phase 4 polling pattern standard.
